Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the "Personal rclone Drive Client" (the "App") handles information it receives from Google. The App is a Google OAuth client used with rclone, an open-source program that runs on the user's own computer. It is operated by an individual for personal use and is not a commercial or public service.

In short: the App's owner never receives your data. rclone runs on your own device and talks to Google directly. Nothing is collected, sold, shared or used to train AI models.

1. Information the App accesses

When you sign in with Google and approve the App, rclone on your device may access:

  • Google Drive files and folders — their contents, names, sizes, dates and other metadata — through the scopes drive, docs and drive.metadata.readonly.
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Google, which prove that you granted permission.

The App does not request your contacts, email messages, location, or any other Google data.

2. How the information is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features you ask rclone to perform on your own device: listing, copying, syncing, backing up, downloading, uploading, mounting and streaming your own Drive files. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

3. Where the information is stored

  • File contents and metadata move directly between Google's servers and the computer running rclone. They are not sent to, or stored on, any server run by the App's owner.
  • The OAuth token is saved only in the rclone configuration file (rclone.conf) on the user's own device. rclone can optionally encrypt this file with a password.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party. The App's owner has no access to it.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

The App's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Artificial intelligence and machine learning

Google user data obtained through the App is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

7. Retention and deletion

The App keeps no copy of your data, so there is nothing for its owner to retain or delete. You stay in control of everything on your own device:

  • To stop access at any time, remove the App at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This immediately invalidates the stored token.
  • To delete the token from your computer, delete the remote with rclone config delete <remote-name> or remove the rclone.conf file.
  • Any files rclone downloaded or cached on your computer stay under your control and can be deleted like any other file.

8. Security

All communication with Google uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Because tokens live only on the user's own device, keeping that device and the rclone.conf file secure is the user's responsibility; encrypting the rclone configuration is recommended.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
